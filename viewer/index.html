Within this finite window, each copy is compared with the copy displaced by v; when +v leaves the display box, −v is compared instead. Green: a local same-frame match. Red: a local mismatch. Grey: neither comparison fits, and grey copies are excluded from the meter. v is in units of the copies currently shown. A genuine whole-space period would make every tested comparison green, but 100% here does not prove a period, and the test is offered only on the 64, 512, 4096 and cousin views, where the window is large enough to mean something.
