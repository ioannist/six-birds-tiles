# Chair44 (R44) — One shape. No periodic tiling.

A single connected solid that tiles three-dimensional space, with no nonzero
translation period in any of its tilings: the Chair44 (R44) construction and proof
submission presented in this repository.

The film starts with an almost identical plain chair that **does** repeat.
Restoring Chair44's tiny bumps and dents breaks that arrangement. We then watch
compatible Chair44 copies form larger patches and follow the paper's hierarchy
argument, which concerns every possible tiling—not only the patch on screen.

Canonical rational geometry and certified integer poses drive the visuals.
Colours are explanatory annotations. Magnified sections preserve proportions.
The film illustrates the repository's proof submission; a finite animation
does not by itself prove a universal statement. Formalization scope, including
the named compiler hooks, is documented in the paper and reader package.
